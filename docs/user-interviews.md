# Freelance finance discovery interviews

Target: three independent professionals who receive work payments through PayPal. Duration: 15 minutes each. Do not request account access or transaction exports.

## Outreach draft

> I'm testing a small tool that explains changes in freelance income by client and type of work, then connects that picture to hours, known costs, and upcoming expenses. Could I ask you a few questions about your last payment or workload decision? It takes 15 minutes; no account access or financial documents needed.

## Questions

1. Tell me about the last time you decided whether to take more work in one area or with one client. What information did you use?
2. When income fell or rose recently, how did you determine the cause? How long did it take?
3. Which payments are still expected in the next month? Where do you track dates and confidence?
4. Which costs or subscriptions can change your decisions? How do you notice them now?
5. If you had an alert about one change in the coming month, what would make it actionable? Which alerts would be noise?
6. Show or describe the current workflow. Ask what they would do if a payment has no clear project or the time spent is unknown.

For each interview, log the last real decision, tools used, time spent, missing data, and whether a concrete FinSig result would have changed the action. Avoid leading with a demo or asking whether they “like” an AI manager.
